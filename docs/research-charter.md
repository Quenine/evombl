# EvoMBL research charter

## Medical problem and research question

Metallo-beta-lactamases contribute to antimicrobial resistance by compromising
beta-lactam treatment. EvoMBL asks which inhibitor chemotypes retain activity across
clinically important, source-verified enzyme variants, under assay conditions that permit
scientifically defensible comparison.

## Novelty and translation

The novelty thesis is that variant-resolved evidence, assay-aware evaluation, and
prospective testing can reveal robustness missed by single-variant analyses. The
translational objective is to nominate experimentally testable chemistry with explicit
evidence lineage, not to substitute computation for laboratory confirmation.

Terms are gated: a **prediction** is a model output; a **computational hit** meets a
predeclared in-silico rule; an **experimental hit** meets a prospective laboratory rule;
a **lead series** has reproducible activity and an acceptable emerging property profile;
and a **development candidate** requires a substantially broader, formally governed
preclinical evidence package. One status never implies the next.

Prospective validation is mandatory for hit claims. Raw data, transformations, protocols,
versions, exclusions, and signed reports must be retained with audit-ready provenance in
support of future FDA-oriented evidence needs. This infrastructure does not itself assert
regulatory compliance. Potentially enabling findings require intellectual-property review
before public disclosure, publication, presentation, or repository release.
