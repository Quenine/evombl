# Competitor landscape

This registry separates investigational inhibitors (taniborbactam, xeruborbactam, and APC148) from approved treatments, preclinical compounds, biochemical probes, and computational predictions. Regulatory and development status must be verified from dated authoritative sources before use. Seed metadata remain pending verification; no status is inferred here. Last reviewed: 2026-07-25.

Other candidates may be added only after manual review of the seed-source citation network.
## Batch 2C1 limitation

Seed-source metadata may identify candidate competitor literature but does not verify clinical phase, mechanism, activity, or comparative performance. Those statements remain provisional pending manual full-text scientific review.
