# Batch 3D2 assay-context bridge readiness

- Sixteen curated Paper 2 observations form four crude IC50 XER/TAN pairs and four purified Ki XER/TAN pairs.
- The four bridge variants are NDM-1, VIM-2, IMP-1, and IMP-10.
- IC50 and Ki remain explicitly distinct assay endpoints.
- No IC50-to-Ki conversion or IC50/Ki fold quotient was calculated.
- Censor thresholds were not treated as exact concentrations.
- NDM-1 has TAN/XER <1 in both contexts; VIM-2 has TAN/XER >1 in both contexts.
- IMP-1 and IMP-10 have TAN/XER >1 supported by lower bounds in both contexts.
- Direction is concordant across contexts for all four selected variants.
- This is descriptive directional concordance, not proof of quantitative equivalence between crude IC50 and purified Ki.
- No correlation, p-value, confidence interval, regression, or mechanistic inference was performed.
- Structural, causal, predictive, hit, and lead claims remain unauthorised.
